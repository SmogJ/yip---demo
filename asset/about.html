Our story

Why Youth Impact Project exists

We started YIP because too many talented young Nigerians were being pushed toward fraud and burnout by the pressures of life online. Here's the problem we saw, and the solution we built.

The problem

Wealth pressure online, real harm offline

Over 75% of Nigerian youths spend 3–10 hours daily on social media, exposed to content that drives anxiety, depression, and the pressure to display success at any cost. That pressure often ends in fraudulent schemes and poor financial decisions — not the future anyone signed up for.

The solution

Legitimate income, taught and mentored

YIP replaces the pull of fraud with something better: freelancing and digital-task income, financial literacy education, entrepreneurship mentorship, and a community that celebrates honest work. Every part of the programme is designed to be accessible from a phone.

Problem

Fraud exposure & anxiety from unregulated online pressure.

Solution

Financial literacy + legitimate income pathways.

Target

Nigerian youths aged 16–25, expandable to 13–25.

Expectation

Thousands earning legitimately within year one.
