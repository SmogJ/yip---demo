Why we exist

Social media pressure is pushing youth toward fraud. We offer a real way out.

The pressure to display wealth online is driving Nigerian youths toward fraudulent schemes and risky financial decisions. Youth Impact Project tackles that head-on — with financial literacy, entrepreneurship training, and safe, traceable income opportunities for youths aged 13–25.

Learn more about YIP
THE PROBLEM

Fraudulent adverts and social comparison online significantly predict depression, anxiety, and risky money habits among young Nigerians.

THE SOLUTION

Mentorship, financial literacy, and legitimate digital income — replacing the pull of "yahoo" schemes with real pathways.

THE TARGET

Students, graduates, freelancers, and underemployed youths aged 16–25 across Nigeria, expandable as we grow.

THE EXPECTATION

Thousands of youths earning legitimately, better financial literacy scores, and real employer partnerships.
